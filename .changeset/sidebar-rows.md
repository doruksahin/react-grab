The sidebar list stays readable with many selections. Each selection is a short row (status, comment, element, ticket, where it is) and its card opens underneath, one at a time. The status chips count the selections the list shows, so "All" matches the rows and tickets opened for a single comment are counted, and a new "Attention" chip shows the flagged ones and the ones not on this page. The list can be grouped by group or by status and ordered with the ones that need attention first; section headers stay at the top while scrolling. Comments on a card start closed unless the card was opened from "Open thread". The search, Filter and Flagged controls are unchanged.
