A consistent overlay look (ADR-0022, step 1)

The dark theme now uses the AdCreative.ai Design System's palette: one violet-tinted surface for the toolbar, labels, sidebar and dialogs, hairline borders, and the system's radius scale. Text uses five named sizes with nothing below 11 px, replacing 7–10 px text. The label header and code text read their colours from tokens. Nothing about behaviour or the DOM contract changes.
