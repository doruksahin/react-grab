Selection cards are redesigned in three zones: a header with the comment, its element, group and time; sections for where the selection is, how the user got there and screenshots; and one main action, "Create Jira ticket". A selection not on the page shows a notice with "Guide me" and "Open page"; a comments row shows only where it is ("Made on /boards"). The replay link, the steps and the Chrome Recorder export are one "How we got here" panel, and repeated steps show once with a count. Moving and removing a selection sit in one ⋯ menu, on cards and on comments rows, and the list's clear control is neutral until hovered. Starting a guide from the sidebar closes the sidebar.
